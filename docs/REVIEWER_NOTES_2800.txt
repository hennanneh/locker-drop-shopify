LockerDrop adds smart-locker pickup as a checkout shipping option: the customer picks a nearby locker, the merchant drops the package in, the customer collects it with a secure link, and the order auto-fulfills on pickup.

BEFORE YOU START
- Carrier-calculated shipping must be on (Development, Advanced and Plus stores have it).
- Embedded app, no separate login.
- Billing is usage-based via Shopify Billing: $1.50-$6.00 per order by locker size, capped at $200 per 30 days. Test charges on a dev store.

STEPS
1. Install and approve data access. The setup guide opens automatically.
2. Setup:
 a. My Lockers: search the map (e.g. ZIP 75212) and select a locker. Save.
 b. Product Sizes: give one product a Small size (or dimensions such as 10 x 6 x 3). A locker is offered only when 2+ of the needed size are free; small units are most available.
 c. Shipping: in the wizard's last step (or Settings > Shipping Setup) click "Add LockerDrop to my shipping rates". The status turns green.
 d. Approve billing: click "Review and approve" in the blue banner, then Approve (test charge).
3. Storefront: add the sized product, checkout, and enter an address within a few miles of the locker. "LockerDrop @ [locker]" appears with distance and pickup date. Select it and pay with Bogus Gateway (card 1).
4. The order email gets "Order confirmed: locker pickup". In LockerDrop > Orders the order shows Pending drop-off.
5. Test orders never reserve a physical locker, so they show reviewer Sim buttons (Actions column and the View panel):
 - Sim drop-off > confirm: Ready for pickup, pickup link created, customer emailed.
 - Sim pickup > confirm: Completed, and the order auto-fulfills in Shopify (open it in Admin to see Fulfilled).
 Sim buttons appear only for test orders or on development stores. They run the real completion path; only the physical locker step is simulated. The demo video shows a real locker cycle.
6. Optional storefront blocks: LockerDrop > Learn > "Add a LockerDrop Block to Your Theme" > "Add to product page". The theme editor opens with the badge placed; click Save. Blocks need an Online Store 2.0 theme; checkout pickup works on any theme.
7. Uninstall to confirm clean removal.
